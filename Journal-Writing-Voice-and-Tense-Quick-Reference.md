## Journal Writing — Voice and Tense Quick Reference

| Journal Section / Situation       | Preferred Voice  | Tense                     | Easy Rule                                     | Example                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **General Scientific Fact**       | Active           | Present                   | Facts → Present                               | Machine learning **is** widely used in healthcare.                                                     |
| **Current State of Knowledge**    | Active           | Present / Present Perfect | Current knowledge → Present / Present Perfect | Several studies **have demonstrated** the importance of machine learning.                              |
| **Introduction**                  | Mostly Active    | Present + Present Perfect | Introduction → Present + Present Perfect      | Machine learning **is** widely used. Several researchers **have investigated** its applications.       |
| **Previous Research**             | Active           | Past / Present Perfect    | Previous research → Past / Present Perfect    | Kumar et al. **reported** improved accuracy.                                                           |
| **Literature Review**             | Mostly Active    | Past + Present Perfect    | Literature Review → Past + Present Perfect    | Kumar et al. **reported** significant results. Several studies **have demonstrated** similar findings. |
| **Research Gap**                  | Active           | Present / Present Perfect | Gap → Present / Present Perfect               | Few studies **have examined** this relationship.                                                       |
| **Research Objective**            | Active           | Present                   | Objective → Present                           | This study **aims** to develop a prediction model.                                                     |
| **Methodology – What You Did**    | Active / Passive | Past                      | Methods → Past                                | We **collected** the data. / The data **were collected**.                                              |
| **Experimental Procedure**        | Often Passive    | Past                      | Procedure → Passive + Past                    | The samples **were analyzed** using the proposed method.                                               |
| **Results**                       | Active / Passive | Past                      | Results → Past                                | The model **achieved** 95% accuracy. / A significant difference **was observed**.                      |
| **Figure / Table Description**    | Active           | Present                   | Figures/Tables → Present                      | Figure 2 **shows** the model architecture.                                                             |
| **Discussion – Specific Result**  | Active / Passive | Past                      | Obtained result → Past                        | The model **achieved** 95% accuracy.                                                                   |
| **Discussion – Interpretation**   | Mostly Active    | Present                   | Interpretation → Present                      | These findings **suggest** that the model is effective.                                                |
| **Conclusion / Implication**      | Mostly Active    | Present                   | Conclusion → Present                          | This study **demonstrates** that the proposed approach is effective.                                   |
| **Future Research / Future Work** | Active           | Future / Modal            | Future → should / may / will                  | Future studies **should investigate** larger datasets.                                                 |

### One-Line Memory Rule

**Facts → Present | Previous Research → Past/Present Perfect | Methods → Past | Results → Past | Interpretation → Present | Conclusion → Present | Future Work → Future/Modal**

# Literature Survey Essentials

| Important Point | Remember |
|---|---|
| **Chronological** | Arrange papers year-wise; show research development |
| **Methodological** | Organize and compare papers by methods/algorithms |
| **Thematic** | Organize papers by topic/theme/problem |
| **One paragraph, one focus** | Don't mix chronological, methodological, and thematic discussion |
| **Keywords** | Keep the literature relevant to your research keywords |
| **Table** | Best for comparing many papers clearly |
| **Paragraph** | Best when a topic is new or needs detailed explanation |
| **Don't just summarize** | Explain what you learned or took from each study |
| **Compare** | Show similarities, differences, strengths, and limitations |
| **Research gap** | Identify what previous studies have not addressed |
| **Connect your work** | Explain how your proposed work addresses the gap |
| **Final literature paragraph** | Research gap → your solution → connection to methodology |

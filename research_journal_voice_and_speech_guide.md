# Voice and Speech Usage in Research Journal Writing

A concise reference for choosing **active voice, passive voice, direct
speech, and indirect speech** in different sections of a research
journal paper.

### Active Voice

Use active voice when the writer, study, method, model, or findings
should be clear and direct.

**Example:**\
\> The proposed model achieved an accuracy of 96.2%.

### Passive Voice

Use passive voice when the **procedure, action, or result** is more
important than the person who performed it.

**Example:**\
\> The features were extracted using RADWT.

### Direct Speech --- Rare in Journal Writing

Direct speech reproduces another person's **exact words**, normally
inside quotation marks. It is uncommon in scientific, engineering, and
biomedical journal articles.

**Example:**\
\> Smith stated, "The proposed method improves classification accuracy."

**Preferred approach:** Unless an exact quotation is necessary, report
the author's finding using indirect speech.

### Indirect Speech

Indirect speech reports **another author's findings without quoting the
author's exact words**.

**Example:**\
\> Smith et al. reported that the proposed method improved
classification accuracy.

Another example:

> Previous studies have shown that wavelet analysis can improve feature
> extraction.

## 2. Key Takeaway

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Writing Style                       When to Use
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Active voice                        Use for clear and direct statements
                                      about your study, method, results,
                                      or interpretation.

  Passive voice                       Use when the procedure, action, or
                                      outcome is more important than who
                                      performed it.

  Direct speech                       Rarely use in scientific journal
                                      writing; use only when an exact
                                      quotation is necessary.

  Indirect speech                     Use when reporting, summarizing, or
                                      comparing findings from other
                                      researchers.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Important:** Active/passive voice and direct/indirect speech are
different grammar concepts. A research paper does not need to use only
one style throughout.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Journal Section --- Main Style and Purpose

  | Journal Section | Main Style to Use | Key Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| *Abstract* | Active + Passive | Summarize what was done and what was found. |
| *Introduction* | Active + Indirect speech | Introduce the topic, research problem, gap, and previous research. |
| *Literature Review**| Indirect speech | Summarize and compare previous researchers' work. |
| *Methodology / Methods* | Passive voice often useful | Explain what was done and how the study was conducted. |
| *Results* | Active + Passive | Report the findings clearly. |
| *Discussion* | Active + Indirect speech | Interpret findings and compare them with previous studies. |
| *Conclusion* | Active voice mainly | Summarize the main findings and implications. |
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Daily Rule to Remember

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Style                               Simple Rule
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Active voice                        **My study / finding → say it
                                      clearly and directly.**

  Passive voice                       **Procedure / action → focus on
                                      what was done.**

  Indirect speech                     **Other researchers → report what
                                      they found without quoting.**

  Direct speech                       **Exact quotation → usually avoid
                                      in scientific journals.**
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### Quick Examples

**Active voice:**\
\> The proposed method reduced classification error.

**Passive voice:**\
\> The samples were analyzed using the proposed method.

**Indirect speech:**\
\> Kumar et al. reported that wavelet-based features improved
classification performance.

**Direct speech --- rarely needed:**\
\> Kumar stated, "Wavelet-based features improved classification
performance."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Quick Memory Formula

**Your findings → Active voice**\
**Your procedure → Passive voice when appropriate**\
**Other researchers' findings → Indirect speech**\
**Exact quotations → Direct speech, rarely used**

> **Note:** These are general academic-writing guidelines rather than
> absolute rules. Always follow the author guidelines of the target
> journal.

### One-Line Memory Rule

> **Describe → Process → Interpret → Compare → Problem/Solution**

### Important Note

These paragraph types are **writing purposes**, not rigid rules. A
paragraph type may appear in more than one journal section depending on
what the paragraph needs to accomplish.
